## Public research and useful principles

Queries sampled: freelance web developer Philippines business website development; web developer Bulacan website redesign; technical SEO services Philippines web performance; custom CMS booking system developer Philippines; web developer Bulacan portfolio; custom CMS Philippines development; booking system developer Philippines.

[CREATE](https://create.com.ph/) presents service scope, inspectable work and a direct project enquiry path; this supports making service evidence and scope easy to find. [Web Developer Philippines](https://webdeveloperphilippines.com/) connects service categories with projects and contact. [SEO Company Philippines' technical service page](https://seocompanyphilippines.com/services/technical-seo/) separates technical implementation from a generic audit. The practical opportunity is clearer verified scope and project evidence, not copying their claims or pricing. These sites appeared in the sample; their traffic, sales and ranking strength were not verified.

The Bulacan-focused sample also surfaced local portfolios including [Rovic De Leon](https://rvcblck.github.io/rovic_portfolio/) and [Renchester Jardiel](https://renchester.vercel.app/). One direct Rovic extraction returned no readable lines; only its search-result positioning was available, so no implementation claims are inferred. Local peers reinforce the usefulness of a clear professional identity and tangible work; they do not justify unsupported local superiority claims.

[Brittany Chiang](https://brittanychiang.com/) is a reference for accessible navigation, concise professional context and project descriptions linked to technology. [Coalition Technologies](https://coalitiontechnologies.com/) is a reference for service/case-study relationships and intent segmentation. Neither is treated as a verified ranking benchmark or a layout/content template. This solo portfolio retains six supported service pages rather than reproducing an agency's extensive taxonomy.

## Next decisions require real data

After publication, compare 28-day and three-month query/page/country/device exports. Prioritize pages with qualified nonbranded impressions, weak snippets relative to position, or indexing failures. Record changes and compare complete periods. Do not publish invented volumes, difficulty, position gains or conversion statistics. New content should solve an identifiable question that the existing pages do not answer; there is no present evidence for extra location or framework landing pages.

Google's [AI feature guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) supports established crawlability and useful-content fundamentals. No specialized AI meta tags, promised citations or speculative ranking mechanism were added. The owner-requested llms.txt is only a generated public directory.
