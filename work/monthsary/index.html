01 / Context

The problem it addresses

A personal dedication site to celebrate and preserve an anniversary — every moment together counted, remembered, and expressed with love.

02 / Build

What the project does

A personal anniversary website dedicated to a 15th monthsary. Features a live time-together counter (days, months, hours, minutes, seconds since May 21 2025), a love timeline, photo museums, photobooth strips, video moments, and handwritten letters. Built with a warm, romantic design and subtle interactions.

03 / Narrative decision

Narrative decision

The interface organizes a personal story through elapsed time, photographs, milestones, and letters rather than presenting them as a conventional gallery.
